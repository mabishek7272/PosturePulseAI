### State of the depth metrics

`static3d.subject_yaw_to_camera` and `static3d.axial_trunk_twist` are fully computed and tested
end to end, but **dormant**: they are not in `references/assessment-battery.v0-draft.json`, so
nothing requests them and `checkResults` would reject an unrequested one. Both are `tier: proxy`,
method `mediapipe_2_5d_depth`, forced to `no_reference` in reports. Records use `module: "static"`.

Measured limits: a 25° subject yaw returns `insufficient_depth_samples` (refused, not wrong);
realistic correlated depth noise drops many frames but survivors stay near truth; the twist sigma
(about 4°) exceeds small effects, which is why it ships as `proxy`.
